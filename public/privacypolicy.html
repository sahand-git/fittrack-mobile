How FitTrack uses step data

When you choose to connect, FitTrack requests permission to read step counts from Apple Health or Android Health Connect. It does not request permission to write health data.

FitTrack uses the count for the selected day to display activity and estimate step calories. Reading again replaces the saved daily total instead of adding it twice. It does not read in the background.

Your profile and logs are saved on this device. Exporting a backup includes your saved logs. If you separately choose server-backed sync, those logs are sent to the server hosting your FitTrack app. An AI coaching request can also include your day's activity summary.

You can revoke step-reading permission in your phone's health settings. You can edit the saved total or remove local data through the app's reset option. Revoking permission does not automatically erase totals already saved.
